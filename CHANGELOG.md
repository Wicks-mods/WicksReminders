# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-06

The first release.

### Added

- Timers, alarms and event reminders, saved across sessions and shared by
  your characters unless one is made for a single character.
- Timers from presets or any typed length, optionally starting again each
  time they end.
- Alarms at a time of day, once or on chosen days, on your computer's
  clock or the realm's.
- Event reminders: logging in, entering a zone, reaching an inn or a city,
  the mailbox, bank, auction house, a vendor or a trainer, the end of the
  next fight, reaching a level, and the daily and raid resets.
- Anything that came due while you were logged out goes off at login,
  marked as missed.
- Alert cards with Snooze and Done that stack near the top of the screen
  and can be dragged. A sound, the words in large letters, a chat line and
  a taskbar flash, each with its own switch. Alarms ring until answered.
- Runs on TBC Classic Anniversary and on Forever.
- `/remind` for quick ones from chat, a page under Wick's Mods, and a line
  in the suite's launcher with the next countdown on broker displays.
